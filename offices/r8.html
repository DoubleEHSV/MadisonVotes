Associate Justice of the Supreme Court, Place 8

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Serves on Alabama’s highest state court, reviewing legal issues rather than conducting a new trial.

What they decide

Decides appeals and petitions within the court’s jurisdiction and participates in rules governing Alabama courts.

Why it matters

Its interpretations guide lower courts and influence how state laws and constitutional protections apply across Alabama.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

February 2024 · Alabama

Interpreting the wrongful-death statute in LePage

In LePage, the court held that the Wrongful Death of a Minor Act applies to frozen embryos outside a uterus, reversing dismissal of the wrongful-death claims.

The role in action: An interpretation of a state statute can have consequences for medical providers and families. This was a civil-liability ruling.

December 2024 · Alabama

Reviewing workers’ compensation limits

In Crenshaw v. Sonic, the court rejected a constitutional challenge to the Workers’ Compensation Act’s exclusive-remedy provisions.

The role in action: Justices decide whether statutory limits on lawsuits comply with the state constitution.
