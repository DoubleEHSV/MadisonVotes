State Senate · KNOW THE ROLE

State Senator, District 8

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Represents a district in the Alabama Senate, one chamber of the state Legislature.

What they decide

Introduces and votes on state laws and budgets, works in committees, and considers appointments requiring Senate confirmation.

Why it matters

State laws and budgets affect schools, health services, public safety, taxes, and local government authority.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

March 2024 · Alabama

Voting on education savings accounts

The Legislature passed the CHOOSE Act before the governor signed it. The measure established education savings accounts.

The role in action: State senators participate in deciding which education proposals become state law; passage requires action by both chambers.

May 2024 · Alabama

Changing workforce and childcare policy

The Legislature passed the Working for Alabama package, including workforce changes and childcare and housing tax credits.

The role in action: Senators debate, amend, and vote on policies that influence employment and access to services. This example concerns the chamber’s work, not a particular district member’s vote.
