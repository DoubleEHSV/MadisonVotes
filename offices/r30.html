Superintendent, Madison County Board of Education

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Leads the daily administration of Madison County Schools and implements the county school board’s policies.

What they decide

Recommends budgets and personnel actions, supervises system operations, and develops plans for the board’s consideration.

Why it matters

These choices affect instruction, staffing, transportation, and school operations. This role serves the county system, which is separate from Huntsville City and Madison City schools.
