Commissioner of Agriculture and Industries

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Leads Alabama’s Department of Agriculture and Industries, which supports agriculture and administers regulatory programs.

What they decide

Directs programs involving animal and plant health, agricultural inspections, weights and measures, and other duties assigned to the department.

Why it matters

These services affect farmers, food and agricultural markets, and the accuracy of everyday commercial measurements.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

December 2024 · Alabama · Cullman and Pickens counties

Responding to a poultry disease outbreak

State agriculture officials and USDA confirmed avian influenza in a commercial broiler farm and a backyard flock and coordinated the response.

The role in action: Department leadership oversees animal-health measures that can affect farms and the spread of disease.
