Court of Criminal Appeals Judge, Place 5

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Reviews criminal appeals, including challenges to convictions and sentences.

What they decide

Examines alleged legal errors in criminal proceedings and decides whether judgments should be affirmed, reversed, or returned for further proceedings.

Why it matters

Its work affects fair trials, lawful punishment, and the rights of defendants and the public.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

March 2023 · Madison County case

Ordering a new trial in the Darby case

The court reversed former Huntsville officer William Darby’s murder conviction because of a jury-instruction issue and ordered a new trial.

The role in action: Appellate judges can require a case to be tried again when legal errors affect the proceedings.
