Madison County License Director

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Leads the county department providing vehicle registration, title, and other licensing services.

What they decide

Oversees staff and delivery of legally required registration and license transactions.

Why it matters

Efficient, accurate service affects everyday tasks such as renewing vehicle tags and maintaining registration records.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

Launching self-service tag-renewal kiosks

The License Department introduced kiosks at two district offices so drivers could renew registration and receive stickers without a service-counter visit.
