United States Senator

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Represents all of Alabama in the United States Senate, one of the two chambers of Congress.

What they decide

Votes on federal laws, spending, and taxes; considers presidential nominations and treaties; and participates in oversight of federal agencies.

Why it matters

Senate decisions affect national programs, federal judges, defense, and the federal resources reaching Alabama.
