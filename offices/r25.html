Madison County Sheriff

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Leads the Madison County Sheriff’s Office, responsible for county law enforcement, the jail, and service of court process.

What they decide

Sets department operations and priorities within the law, supervises personnel, and oversees investigations, patrol, and detention services.

Why it matters

These responsibilities affect public safety, treatment of people in custody, and how residents experience law enforcement.

Training deputies for mental-health crises
