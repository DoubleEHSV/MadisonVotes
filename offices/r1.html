Lieutenant Governor

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Presides over the Alabama Senate and succeeds to the governorship if that office becomes vacant.

What they decide

Handles Senate presiding duties under its rules and serves on or makes appointments to boards where law provides.

Why it matters

The office connects Senate procedure, public boards, and continuity of executive leadership.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

March 2022 · Alabama

Supporting military-family licensing changes

Six bills signed in 2022 expanded occupational licensing flexibility for military spouses. Lieutenant Governor Will Ainsworth discussed the Military Stability Commission’s work on the package.

The role in action: Chairing a commission can help an officeholder coordinate proposals; the Legislature and governor still enact the laws.
