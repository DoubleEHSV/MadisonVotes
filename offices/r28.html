Madison County Coroner

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Investigates unattended, suspicious, and other deaths that fall within the coroner’s authority.

What they decide

Directs death investigations and coordinates with forensic and law-enforcement professionals to establish the circumstances of a death.

Why it matters

Reliable findings provide answers for families and inform death records, public health, and criminal investigations.
