Circuit Court Judge, 23rd Judicial Circuit, Place 2

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Presides over trials in the 23rd Judicial Circuit, serving Madison County.

What they decide

Makes legal and procedural rulings in cases assigned to the judge, including serious criminal and civil matters. Circuit courts also hear certain appeals from lower courts.

Why it matters

A judge’s handling of evidence, procedure, and legal disputes affects fair hearings and the resolution of major cases.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

October 2023 · Madison County

Accepting a plea and imposing a sentence

After his murder conviction was reversed, former Huntsville officer William Darby pleaded guilty to manslaughter and received a sentence including probation.

The role in action: A circuit judge handles plea and sentencing decisions under the law; this is a different stage from appellate review.

Ruling on an employer’s medical-benefit obligation
