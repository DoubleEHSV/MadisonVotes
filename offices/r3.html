United States Representative, 5th Congressional District

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Represents Alabama’s 5th Congressional District in the U.S. House of Representatives.

What they decide

Votes on federal legislation and spending, conducts oversight, and can introduce revenue bills. The House has the power to impeach federal officials.

Why it matters

The representative gives this district a voice on national policy and helps constituents navigate federal agencies.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.
