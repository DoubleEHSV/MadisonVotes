State Auditor

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Accounts for state-owned personal property, such as equipment, and checks agency property inventories.

What they decide

Oversees property accountability and performs duties on state boards assigned to the office.

Why it matters

Accurate inventories help protect taxpayer-funded assets. This office has a distinct role from the state’s Examiners of Public Accounts.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

Fiscal year 2024 · Alabama

Reporting missing state equipment

The auditor’s annual loss report lists agency property reported lost, stolen, or otherwise missing, with explanations and values.

The role in action: Publishing these findings makes missing public assets visible to other officials and the public. A missing item is not, by itself, proof of theft.

Checking agency inventories
