District Court Judge, Madison County, Place 5

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Serves in Madison County’s state district court, a trial court with limited jurisdiction.

What they decide

Handles assigned matters such as misdemeanors, preliminary felony proceedings, small claims, and other civil cases within the court’s authority.

Why it matters

These courts handle many residents’ first encounters with the justice system, making fair procedure and timely decisions consequential.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

March 2025 · Madison County

Deciding whether a felony case can advance

After a preliminary hearing in the Xavier Cole case, a district judge found probable cause and sent the case to a grand jury.

The role in action: The judge screens the evidence at an early stage. A probable-cause ruling is not a finding of guilt.
