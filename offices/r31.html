County Education · KNOW THE ROLE

Member, Madison County Board of Education, District 3

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Serves with other members on the governing board for Madison County Schools.

What they decide

Votes on system policies, the budget, facilities, and personnel recommendations within the board’s authority.

Why it matters

Board decisions shape school resources, priorities, and accountability. A member acts through board votes; daily administration belongs to the superintendent.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

October 2024 · Madison County Schools

Approving a new school’s identity

The board voted to name the system’s new school Sparkman Intermediate, a project intended to serve grades four through six.

The role in action: Board votes help shape school facilities and how they serve the community. The superintendent and staff handle implementation.
