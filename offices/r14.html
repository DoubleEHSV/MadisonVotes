State Treasurer

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Manages and safeguards Alabama’s financial resources.

What they decide

Oversees investment and cash management, state debt service, trust funds, savings programs, and unclaimed property administration.

Why it matters

Good stewardship affects the safety and earnings of public money and services that help families save or reclaim funds.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

2023 results, announced January 2024 · Alabama

Returning unclaimed money

Treasury reported returning $50.7 million in unclaimed property to Alabama residents during 2023.

The role in action: The treasurer administers the claims process that reunites people with assets held by the state.
