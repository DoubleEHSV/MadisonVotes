Governor

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Leads Alabama’s executive branch and carries out state laws.

What they decide

Signs or vetoes legislation, proposes a budget, appoints officials where authorized, and directs executive agencies.

Why it matters

These choices shape statewide services, spending priorities, and how laws are put into practice.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

March 2024 · Alabama

Signing the CHOOSE Act

Governor Kay Ivey signed HB129, creating education savings accounts for eligible education expenses.

The role in action: A governor can turn a bill passed by the Legislature into law by signing it.
