Attorney General

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Serves as Alabama’s chief legal officer and represents the state in legal matters.

What they decide

Sets litigation priorities within the office’s authority, handles state appeals and legal advice, and pursues authorized criminal and consumer-protection enforcement.

Why it matters

The office’s legal decisions affect enforcement of state law, protection against fraud, and Alabama’s position in major lawsuits.
