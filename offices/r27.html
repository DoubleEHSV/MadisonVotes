Madison County Tax Collector

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Collects and distributes Madison County property taxes to the public bodies entitled to receive them.

What they decide

Oversees tax billing, payment administration, and collection processes under state law.

Why it matters

Accurate collection funds public services and helps residents resolve tax-payment questions. Valuation is handled by the assessor’s office.
