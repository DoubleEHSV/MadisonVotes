Court of Civil Appeals Judge, Place 5

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Reviews appeals in assigned civil matters, including family law, workers’ compensation, and certain administrative cases.

What they decide

Examines the trial record and legal arguments to decide whether a lower decision should stand or be corrected.

Why it matters

These rulings affect family disputes, workplace injury claims, and consistent application of civil law.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

August 2024 · Alabama · Macon County case

Deciding responsibility for medical benefits

In Victoryland v. Arnold, the court upheld a ruling that left the employer responsible for future medical expenses from a work injury despite a later car accident.

The role in action: Appellate judges determine whether trial courts correctly applied workers’ compensation law.

September 2024 · Alabama · Shelby County case

Reviewing a custody judgment

In Robinson v. Robinson, the court affirmed a divorce judgment that included custody and visitation arrangements.

The role in action: Judges review the record and legal arguments rather than retrying the family’s case from the beginning.
