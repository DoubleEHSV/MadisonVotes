Secretary of State

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Serves as Alabama’s chief election official and maintains important state and business records.

What they decide

Administers statewide election responsibilities and campaign-finance filings, oversees records services, and carries out document certification duties.

Why it matters

The office affects access to election information, reliable public records, and the ability to form and document businesses.
