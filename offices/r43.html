State Representative, District 25

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Represents a district in the Alabama House of Representatives.

What they decide

Introduces bills, votes on state laws and budgets, and reviews proposals in legislative committees. Most bills must pass both chambers to become law.

Why it matters

The representative helps set the rules and funding priorities for services used by Madison County residents.
