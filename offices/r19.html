State Education · KNOW THE ROLE

Member, State Board of Education, District 8

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Represents an education district on the Alabama State Board of Education, which sets statewide public K–12 education policy.

What they decide

Votes with the board on academic standards and education rules and selects the state superintendent.

Why it matters

These policies help shape what students learn and how public schools are held accountable. Local boards handle local school-system decisions.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

Setting the third-grade reading benchmark

The board approved a reading-test score used in deciding whether third graders met the promotion standard under the state’s reading law.
