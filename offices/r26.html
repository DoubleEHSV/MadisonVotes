Madison County Tax Assessor

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Identifies and values taxable property in Madison County using state requirements.

What they decide

Oversees property records, appraisal work, and assessment administration. Property values are subject to the applicable review and appeal process.

Why it matters

Assessments help determine property-tax bills and distribute the tax burden among property owners.

November 2019 · Madison County

Providing records in a fire-funding debate

During a WAFF investigation into volunteer-fire funding, the assessor supplied records showing revenue from the county’s three-mill fire tax and explained how exemptions and corrections can change the totals.

The role in action: The assessor’s records help residents examine public funding claims. The County Commission, rather than the assessor, votes on department budgets.
