Public Service Commission, Place 2

A quick look at the job behind the name on your ballot.

What this office does

Serves on the commission regulating utilities within Alabama’s state regulatory jurisdiction.

What they decide

Votes with other commissioners on regulated utility rates and service matters.

Why it matters

Its decisions can affect utility costs and reliability. Electric cooperatives, municipal systems, and TVA are outside its electric rate jurisdiction, an important distinction in the Huntsville area.

Real-world examples

Two ways this role has made a difference. Examples may involve past officeholders or other Alabama districts; each identifies the place and date.

February 2023 · Alabama Power customers

Approving a utility refund

The commission approved a $62 million refund for eligible Alabama Power customers, to be credited on August bills.

The role in action: Commission votes can affect what regulated utility customers pay. This does not cover TVA or municipal electric customers.
